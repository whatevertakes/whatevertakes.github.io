# Our research direction

*2026-11-24 · WhateverTakes*

WhateverTakes is establishing two complementary research tracks: infrastructure learning and vulnerability discovery.

We aim to understand how systems are assembled and where their trust boundaries lie, then investigate those boundaries through authorized experiments, prior research, code review and appropriate tooling.

Our public work will distinguish educational notes, reproduced findings and original discoveries. Evidence, limitations and responsible disclosure matter more than the number of posts or unsupported claims.

This entry describes our direction, not a claim of completed vulnerability research.
